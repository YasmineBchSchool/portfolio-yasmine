About me

Hi, I'm Yasmine! I'm a Communication & Multimedia Design student with a background in software development.

I originally started in tech, but along the way I discovered that I'm more interested in the creative and strategic side of things. I enjoy understanding people, exploring ideas and figuring out how to turn research into meaningful designs.

My main interests are branding, strategy and research, but I also have experience in UI/UX design and a growing interest in AI.

I'm naturally curious, always looking to learn something new and open to exploring different creative directions.
